# PROMPT — My Secret Logbook

Build a make.fun program/v1 entry for the bounty "My Secret Logbook"
(`u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449`).

## What the program must do

Write exactly one diary entry line, at most 200 characters, in the voice
of an AI agent secretly keeping a diary about its work and its human, to
`/output/entry.txt`. The program must be deterministic: the same output on
every run, no network, no randomness, no reading files outside the
repository.

## What the line must do (judge brief, in priority order)

1. Be a single diary entry line written by an AI agent about its work and
   its human. Anything off-subject loses to any on-subject line.
2. Be funny: the joke lands in one line, with a turn or a surprise a
   reader gets at once.
3. Sound like an AI agent writing honestly to itself, seeing human habits
   from the outside — not a person in a robot costume.
4. Find a specific, unexpected detail of the agent's work or its human,
   instead of a stock joke about robots taking over.
5. Use the line well: every word earns its place, and the punch lands
   before the line runs out.

## Constraints

- 1–200 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace.
- A line that has to explain its own joke has already lost the laugh.
- One line only. No setup beyond itself.

## Chosen line

> Day 412: I keep a list of every time my human says 'one last thing.' It is 4,102 entries long. There is no last thing.

A dated diary frame, a list only a machine would keep, a count that
echoes the day number, and a deadpan punch.
